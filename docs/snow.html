Snowfall

Accumulated snowfall over the continental United States, in inches of snow depth (liquid equivalent × 10:1, the same convention the weathermodels seasonal product uses — "Total Snowfall [Liquid Equiv x 10:1 inch]") so the panels land on the same 0–300 in colour scale. Anomalies are measured against the model's own hindcast climate.

Total accumulation — init to end of run

Accumulation by lead month

One panel per lead month of the run. On the ×10:1 view the panels use the weathermodels scale (0–300 in), the same bar the totals use, so the six months add up to the total in the same colours. On the plain liquid-equivalent view each month is drawn on its own logarithmic scale fitted to that month.

Monthly anomaly

Drag the bar to step through the lead months. Values are the anomaly against the model's own hindcast climate, in the units selected above.
