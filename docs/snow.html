Snowfall

Accumulated snowfall over the continental United States, in inches, on a 0–300 in colour scale. Every model's season is shown over the same window, ending 01 July 2027. Anomalies are measured against the model's own hindcast climate.

Total accumulation — init to end of run

Accumulation by lead month

One panel per lead month of the run. Every month uses the same 0–300 in scale as the totals, so the months add up to the total in the same colours. No snowfall is white.

Monthly anomaly

Drag the bar to step through the lead months. Values are the anomaly against the model's own hindcast climate, in the units selected above.
