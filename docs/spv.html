Polar Cap (60–90°N) Geopotential Height Anomaly

A time-height section of the polar cap: the area-weighted mean geopotential height from 60°N to the pole, on a set of pressure levels, with the anomaly divided by its own 1991–2020 standard deviation. The pressure axis is logarithmic, so the stratosphere at the top has the room it needs, and the section runs down to the surface at the bottom.

The left of the dashed line is ERA5 reanalysis — what actually happened. The right is the seasonal forecast — what might happen. The dotted “now” line marks today, so the model’s own past days and the days it is still forecasting are told apart.
