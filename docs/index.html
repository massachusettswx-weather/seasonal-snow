Step 1 — choose a seasonal model

Each model is shown from its own most recent init (ECMWF SEAS5 from 1 November 2026, the other centres from 1 October 2026 — their November runs are not released yet). Pick a model and you will be taken to its list of variables — accumulated snowfall over the continental United States, and the 10 hPa zonal-mean zonal wind (the stratospheric polar vortex proxy) against the ERA5 1991–2020 climatology.

Snowfall is accumulated water equivalent in inches. Anomalies are computed against each model's own hindcast climate. The 10 hPa charts show every ensemble member, the ensemble mean, the ERA5 daily climatology with 10–90th and 30–70th percentile bands, the 1959–2020 record bounds and the model's own climate mean where one could be built.
