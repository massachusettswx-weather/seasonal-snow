ENSO

Sea surface temperature anomaly in each of the four ENSO regions, against the 1991–2020 climatology of the same basin and month. The thick red line is the ensemble mean, the light red lines are the members, the shaded envelope is the member spread, and the black line in front is the observed analysis — NOAA’s ERSST v6, the dataset NOAA/CPC builds the ONI and the RONI from. ONI is the plain anomaly; RONI has the tropical-mean (20°S–20°N) SST anomaly subtracted and is rescaled to the ONI’s own spread, so it says whether the Pacific is warm or cold relative to the rest of the ocean. The forecast is re-levelled onto the analysis, so the plume continues from the observation instead of starting a degree above it.
