Polar Cap (60-90N) Geopotential Height Anomaly

A time-height section of the polar cap: the area-weighted mean geopotential height from 60°N to the pole, on a set of pressure levels, with the anomaly divided by its own 1991–2020 standard deviation. The pressure axis is logarithmic and the section runs from the stratosphere at the top to the surface at the bottom. On the left of the dashed line is the analysis; on the right is the model’s own forecast, running to the end of that model’s run.
